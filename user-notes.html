Data source

Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings (ASHE).

Download the data

ASHE data can be downloaded from the ASHE Headline Statistics for Northern Ireland publication page.

Data quality and suppression

ASHE estimates are based on a sample and are therefore subject to sampling variability. The coefficient of variation (CV) provides an indication of the precision of an estimate. A smaller CV indicates greater precision.

Estimates with a CV greater than 20% are suppressed from publication on quality grounds.

Quality assurance

ASHE statistics have Accredited Official Statistics status. This means they meet the standards of trustworthiness, quality and public value set out in the Code of Practice for Statistics. They have been independently reviewed by the Office for Statistics Regulation.
