Information about this dashboard

This dashboard presents results from the Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings (ASHE) for Northern Ireland. It allows users to explore employee earnings and hours worked by sex, working pattern and geographical area.


Figures are based on employees' place of work and represent gross earnings before deductions.


More information can be found on the User Notes page.
